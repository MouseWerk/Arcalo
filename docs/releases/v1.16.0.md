Arcalo 1.16.0

## Improved

- Hours look the same everywhere: start page, time tracking, reviews, briefing, projects, the calendar and focus sessions follow „Stunden als“ (1,50 h or 1:30 h).
- German shortcut names as on German keyboards: „Strg“, „Umschalt“, „Eingabe“, „Entf“ in tooltips, menus and settings.
- A new German workspace keeps its daily notes in „Tagesnotizen“; existing workspaces keep their „Journal“.
- Consistent wording: „Computer“ everywhere, error messages that say what failed, one week heading format, „Tagesnotiz eines Tages öffnen“ and „Kalender“ can be told apart, „Zusammenfassen“ in both reviews; in English „Import“ and „Apply“ instead of „take over“, „Time tracking“, „Plan the week's bookings“ and „Mon, Tue, Wed“.
- Text that is cut shows in full as a tooltip (tree, search, calendar, tasks, time entries).
- Meetings show their calendar color as a dot in „Termine buchen“ and on the start page.
- Projekte: the table fits its card; schedule and progress move under the name in narrow windows.
- Kalender: day headers wrap instead of cutting icons; booked time stays readable in narrow columns; month cells keep the meeting titles; the start page timeline shows narrow meetings as color only.
- Start page tasks keep their title readable; the page name gives way first.
- Tag suggestions are quiet gray chips; more than two collapse into „+n weitere“.
- Split panes: page titles scale with the pane, no sideways scrollbars, no half-hidden tabs; time tracking and the daily review keep their buttons compact.
- Mermaid labels sit on the page background; query tables show [[links]] as links with a narrow checkbox column; search snippets keep code like `!==` as written.
- Better contrast: today's date markers, the keyboard focus on the current row and dimmed rows (other months, switched-off calendars, done tasks) reach WCAG AA in all themes.
- The assistant panel shows the AI setup hint only once and no suggestions while no AI is connected.

## Fixed

- German texts in the English interface: page counts of PDFs and tags, the quick search booking notice.
- Ellipsis without a space in all menu items and progress texts („Board exportieren…“).
- The graph says „1 Seite“ and „1 Verknüpfung“; the briefing tile reads „noch offen, 2 heute insgesamt“.
- A tooltip no longer stays on screen after the view changed under the pointer.
- The empty graph shows its message as one centered block; the intro scene „Zeit“ stays one board in small windows.
- URLs in mono fields no longer show as „http: //“.
