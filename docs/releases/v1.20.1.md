Arcalo 1.20.1

## Improved

- The description of Git sync in the settings names the services the setup offers: GitHub, GitLab, Gitea and Bitbucket.
- „Mehr in der Doku“ in the network settings and at „Datenbankverschlüsselung“ opens their pages in the new IT section of the documentation.

## Fixed

- Linux: audio files embedded in notes, such as the recordings of voice notes, play in the app and can be wound forward, instead of showing „Das Audio lässt sich hier nicht abspielen“.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Open a voice note with a recording: the player shows the length, plays, and jumping to the middle works. Do the same with an MP3 or WAV dragged into a note.
- Settings → Netzwerk and Settings → Sicherheit → „Datenbankverschlüsselung“: „Mehr in der Doku“ opens the matching page on arcalo.mousewerk.de in the app's language.
