Annalo 1.3.0

## New

- **Windows installer in Annalo's design**: the welcome and finish pages show the Annalo logo on the dark brand background, every page header shows the app icon, and the installer and uninstaller use the Annalo icon. German and English.
- **AI providers**: next to LiteLLM, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter, LM Studio, vLLM) and Ollama.
  - Ollama on this computer is found automatically; its models are listed and missing ones can be downloaded.
  - Each tier (Lokal, Standard, Reasoning) and the embedding model use their own provider and model.
  - `#privat` and "nur lokal" content only goes to providers marked local, also when a provider fails and another takes over.
  - "Verbindung testen" checks each provider step by step: reachable, key, chat, tools, embeddings.
  - Costs come from an editable price table when the provider does not report them; local providers are free.
  - Existing LiteLLM setups keep working without changes.
- **Presentation mode**: "Präsentieren" in the page menu, in Ctrl+K or with Ctrl+Shift+P shows the note full screen as slides, split at `---` (or at `#` headings without rules).
  - Arrow keys, Space, PageUp/PageDown, clicks, Home/End and a number + Enter navigate; Esc ends and restores the window.
  - Counter, progress bar, timer with an optional target duration, and a white "Beamer" look.
  - Speaker notes in a `> [!notiz]` callout or a paragraph starting with `Notiz:` are hidden on the slides and shown in the presenter view: its own window on a second monitor (slides on the other one), an overlay with one monitor.
  - Long slides are scaled down, never cut; images, drawings, PDF cards, tables, code, callouts and tasks are shown read-only.
- **Focus sessions**: 25 or 50 minutes (or your own length) on a Vorgang with a goal, then a 5 or 10 minute break.
  - Started from the ribbon, the timesheet, the Projekte table, a page with `vorgang:` or Ctrl+K; the status bar shows a ring with the time left.
  - A finished session books its minutes as a draft entry on the Vorgang (the goal as description, marked "Fokus"); later sessions of the day with the same goal extend it. Breaks are not booked; aborting asks whether to book the minutes so far.
  - During a session messages and desktop notifications wait and are summed up afterwards; "Pause" is announced without a sound. Sessions survive a restart.
  - Start page widget "Fokus" and the line "Fokus heute: …" in the daily note (at the end of the day or on demand).
- **Activity**: a timeline of pages created and edited (per page and hour, with the characters changed), tasks added and done, bookings, releases, exports, files, focus sessions, backups and Git syncs.
  - Day summary, ranges, "Was habe ich am … gemacht?", filters by type, project/Netzplan/Vorgang and person (`@name`, `owner:`), and a search. A click opens the page or the timesheet.
  - Earlier history is derived once from pages, versions, time entries and attachments.
  - The assistant reads it with the tool "Aktivität" ("Was habe ich am Dienstag gemacht?").
