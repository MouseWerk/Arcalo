Annalo 1.6.0

## New

- **Window backdrop with opacity (Windows 11)**: Einstellungen → Darstellung „Hintergrundeffekt“ offers Mica or Acrylic (off by default), and the new „Deckkraft“ slider (40–100 %, default 80 %) sets how much the theme covers it. It applies live while you drag and works with every color theme, light and dark, including your own; the sidebar stays a little more see-through than the content, text keeps its colors, and at 100 % the window is solid. Settings with Mica switched on keep it. Windows 10, macOS and Linux keep a solid window.
- **More than the default Outlook calendar**: Einstellungen → Kalender → Outlook → „Kalender auswählen“ lists every calendar of Outlook Classic: sub-calendars of your own calendar, calendars of further mailboxes and shared mailboxes in your profile, PST files, calendars colleagues shared with you, room and group calendars from the navigation pane. Tick the ones you want; each gets its own color, its status after every sync („12 Termine · synchronisiert vor 2 Min.“ or what went wrong) and badges for the default calendar, shared calendars and calendars shared as „nur Frei/Gebucht“ (those show their busy times without subjects). A calendar that cannot be opened says why and never stops the others from syncing. „Kalender einer Person öffnen“ adds a colleague's calendar by name or address when it is not in Outlook's navigation pane.
- **One legend, one meeting**: the Kalender shows a legend with every calendar in its color; a click hides or shows a calendar in the view (it keeps syncing), and the start page's „Termine“ follows it. A meeting that is in two selected calendars appears once, from your own calendar, and its details say „Auch in: …“.
- **Colleagues' meetings are not your bookings**: meetings from calendars shared by others are left out of „Woche vorschlagen“, the Tagesrückblick and the quick capture's „Jetzt“ by default. „Für Buchungsvorschläge verwenden“ switches that per calendar. Your default calendar keeps everything it had: booked meetings, meeting notes, „nicht buchen“ marks and the remembered Vorgänge.

## Fixed

- **No see-through lines with Mica**: the thin strip between the sidebar and the pages and between the pages and the side panel let the desktop show through completely. The window now has one tinted layer under all panels, so splitters, borders, the split view divider and the room around the ribbon look like the panels beside them, focused or not. Switching the effect off now takes it off the window right away instead of after a restart.
