Annalo 1.6.0

## New

- **Backups on network shares and in cloud folders**: Einstellungen → Sicherung → „Weitere Sicherungsziele“ adds one or more folders that receive a copy of every backup: a Windows share (`\\server\freigabe\Annalo`, typed or chosen), a mapped drive (`Z:\Sicherung`), a mounted NAS on macOS (`/Volumes/…`) or Linux (`/mnt/…`), or a OneDrive or Nextcloud folder. The local backup stays; „Lokal nur die neueste Sicherung behalten“ lets the destinations replace it.
- **Robust copies**: Annalo backs up locally first and copies in the background. Each copy is written under a temporary name, flushed to the disk, checked for its size and then renamed, with its SHA-256 checksum next to it (`.sha256`). A synced cloud folder therefore never uploads half a file.
- **Offline shares never block Annalo**: when the VPN is off or the share does not answer, the destination shows „Ausstehend“ and Annalo tries again (after a minute, five minutes, then every 15 minutes and with every backup). A share that hangs is given up after a minute without progress; other destinations, saving and quitting are not held up. You are only warned once a destination has missed backups for a day or three backups in a row.
- **Jetzt testen**: writes, reads back and deletes a test file in the destination and shows how long it took. Missing access names the folder and suggests opening it once in Explorer (or Finder) to sign in; Annalo never stores share passwords.
- **Several computers, one share**: every computer writes into its own subfolder, and each destination keeps its own number of backups (and optionally a maximum age). Only Annalo's own backups with their checksum file are ever deleted; other files in the folder stay untouched.
- **Attachments along**: backups now also cover changed attachments (a drawing saved again), and each destination can take the attachments folder and the Markdown copy along. A backup contains the complete database (pages, versions, tasks, time tracking, calendar, settings) and the attachments; credentials and logs are not part of it.
- **Restore from the list**: Einstellungen → Sicherung lists the local backups and those in the destinations with their source. „Wiederherstellen…“ checks the checksum, copies the backup into the data folder and restarts; the previous state is kept as `workspace.db.before-restore-…`. The start-up recovery of a damaged database also uses the backups in reachable destinations.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

1. UNC share: add `\\server\freigabe\Annalo`, „Jetzt testen“, „Jetzt sichern“; the backup and its `.sha256` appear in `\\server\freigabe\Annalo\<computer>\`.
2. Mapped drive: the same with `Z:\Annalo`, also with a folder name containing spaces and umlauts (`Z:\Team Büro\Annalo`).
3. VPN off: „Jetzt sichern“ returns at once, the destination shows „Ausstehend – nicht erreichbar“, Annalo stays responsive and quits normally; after reconnecting, „Erneut versuchen“ (or the next 15-minute retry) copies the backup.
4. Share read-only or without permission: „Jetzt testen“ reports the missing access with the path and the hint to open it once in Explorer.
5. Two PCs on the same share: both write into their own subfolders; with „Behalten 2“ neither deletes the other's backups.
6. OneDrive folder: the destination shows „Cloud-Ordner · OneDrive“ with the hint; OneDrive only ever uploads complete `annalo-….db` files (no `.partial`).
7. Long paths: a destination deep enough that `<computer>\annalo-….db` exceeds 260 characters still works.
8. Restore: „Wiederherstellen…“ on a backup from the share restarts with that state; `workspace.db.before-restore-…` is in the data folder.
