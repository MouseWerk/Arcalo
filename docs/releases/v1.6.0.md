Annalo 1.6.0

## New

- **Window backdrop with opacity (Windows 11)**: Einstellungen → Darstellung „Hintergrundeffekt“ offers Mica or Acrylic (off by default), and the new „Deckkraft“ slider (40–100 %, default 80 %) sets how much the theme covers it. It applies live while you drag and works with every color theme, light and dark, including your own; the sidebar stays a little more see-through than the content, text keeps its colors, and at 100 % the window is solid. Settings with Mica switched on keep it. Windows 10, macOS and Linux keep a solid window.
- **More than the default Outlook calendar**: Einstellungen → Kalender → Outlook → „Kalender auswählen“ lists every calendar of Outlook Classic: sub-calendars of your own calendar, calendars of further mailboxes and shared mailboxes in your profile, PST files, calendars colleagues shared with you, room and group calendars from the navigation pane. Tick the ones you want; each gets its own color, its status after every sync („12 Termine · synchronisiert vor 2 Min.“ or what went wrong) and badges for the default calendar, shared calendars and calendars shared as „nur Frei/Gebucht“ (those show their busy times without subjects). A calendar that cannot be opened says why and never stops the others from syncing. „Kalender einer Person öffnen“ adds a colleague's calendar by name or address when it is not in Outlook's navigation pane.
- **One legend, one meeting**: the Kalender shows a legend with every calendar in its color; a click hides or shows a calendar in the view (it keeps syncing), and the start page's „Termine“ follows it. A meeting that is in two selected calendars appears once, from your own calendar, and its details say „Auch in: …“.
- **Colleagues' meetings are not your bookings**: meetings from calendars shared by others are left out of „Woche vorschlagen“, the Tagesrückblick and the quick capture's „Jetzt“ by default. „Für Buchungsvorschläge verwenden“ switches that per calendar. Your default calendar keeps everything it had: booked meetings, meeting notes, „nicht buchen“ marks and the remembered Vorgänge.
- **An intro on first start**: a short tour of about half a minute shows what Annalo does, with notes and links, `/zeit` and the week, the private assistant, the calendar with „Woche vorschlagen“, quick capture and where your data lives. It plays by itself, the arrow keys move between scenes, Space or hovering pauses it, and Esc or „Überspringen“ goes straight on. With reduced motion it shows calm, static slides.
- **Setup that configures the app**: after the intro, ten short steps ask for the language (preselected from your system), light or dark and a color theme, your working days, daily target and rounding, whether you book time in SAP at all, how to start (sample data, an Obsidian vault or empty) and where the data folder is, AI (none, a local Ollama found automatically, or your company's LiteLLM, OpenAI-compatible or Azure server with a connection test), Outlook or an ICS calendar, Git sync, the backup folder and the desktop (start with the system, close to the tray, the quick capture shortcut with „Jetzt ausprobieren“). Every answer is saved at once, every step can be skipped, and „Fertig“ sums everything up with links back to each step and a few tips.
- **„Zeiterfassung verwenden“**: if you do not book time, one switch (in the setup or under Einstellungen → Zeiterfassung) hides the timesheet, projects and their commands. Booked time is kept.
- **Upgrading from 1.5**: your workspace opens as before; a one-time message „Neu in 1.6: Einführung ansehen“ offers the tour. Einstellungen → Über has „Einführung erneut starten“ (also in the command palette), which starts from your current settings, and „Einrichtung zurücksetzen“, which only clears the note that the intro was seen, never your notes.
- **A start page you arrange yourself**: the start page is a grid of twelve columns. „Anpassen“ turns on edit mode: drag a widget by its header to move it, drag its corner to resize it (it snaps to the grid, the others make room) or pick S, M, L or XL. Outside edit mode there are no handles, only the widgets.
- **Keyboard and screen reader**: in edit mode every widget can be focused; the arrow keys move it, Shift and the arrow keys resize it, Enter opens its settings, Ctrl+D duplicates and Delete removes it. The new position is read out („Uhr: Spalte 1, Zeile 14, 4 × 5“).
- **Boards as tabs**: „Heute“ and „Projekte“ to start with, and as many of your own as you like, empty or from a template. Rename a tab by double-clicking it, reorder it with its menu or Alt+arrow keys, delete it with its menu. The start page remembers the tab you had open.
- **Templates**: „Tagesstart“, „Projektleitung“ and „Minimal“ fill a board in one click; „Auf Standard zurücksetzen“ brings a board back to its template. A board can be exported as a JSON file and imported again, with the text of its notes.
- **Widget gallery**: „Widget hinzufügen“ shows every widget with a small preview, grouped and searchable; Enter adds the first match at the first free place. Each widget has its own settings (the gear, also on hover outside edit mode), its own title, and can be duplicated.
- **Heute, the daily overview**: greeting and date with the calendar week, today's meetings on a timeline with a line for now, the meeting running now or the next one with a countdown and a „Beitreten“ button, the hours booked against your daily target as a ring with what is still missing, the timer (start on one of your last Vorgänge, stop), tasks due and overdue with checkboxes and a field for new ones, your focus sessions and quick actions for the daily note, quick capture, „Woche vorschlagen“ and the Tagesrückblick. Every part can be switched off in its settings.
- **New widgets**: Termine (the next days from your calendars, per calendar, with „Beitreten“ and „Buchen“ for meetings that are over), Aufgaben (by due date, tag, page and priority, tick off inline, add new ones), Zeit diese Woche (per day with the target line or per WBS, gaps link to the Zeiterfassung), Budget (the most critical or the ones you choose, with a burn bar and when the budget runs out at the pace of the last four weeks), Projekt (one Netzplan: budget, linked notes, open tasks and the next meetings), Angeheftet, Seite einbetten (a page to read, always up to date), Aktivität, Links (the ribbon's links or one link group), Wochenvorschlag (what is still open this week), Uhr, Tagesrückblick of the last workday and KI-Vorschläge (built on your computer; only a click asks the assistant). The Notiz can now also show its Markdown or write into a page of its own.
- **Your own widgets with Abfrage**: pick a source (pages, tasks, bookings or meetings), write a filter such as `#projekt status: offen`, `fällig: woche prio>=1` or `zeitraum: monat netzplan: NP-8801`, and show the result as a list, a table, a number or a bar chart. The settings show what Annalo understood and a live preview.
- **Fast with large workspaces**: all widgets in view get their data in one call, widgets further down load when they scroll into view, and the start page follows new bookings, saved pages, ticked tasks, calendar syncs and focus sessions by itself. Narrow panes and split view show the same board in fewer columns.
- **Your start page comes along**: the widgets of your current start page move onto the board „Heute“ with the same order, widths and note.

## Fixed

- **No see-through lines with Mica**: the thin strip between the sidebar and the pages and between the pages and the side panel let the desktop show through completely. The window now has one tinted layer under all panels, so splitters, borders, the split view divider and the room around the ribbon look like the panels beside them, focused or not. Switching the effect off now takes it off the window right away instead of after a restart.
