Arcalo 1.8.0

## New

- **Focus blocks („Fokusblöcke“, time blocking)**: drag a task from the Aufgaben list or a task widget of the start page, or an issue from the Issues page or a Jira widget, onto the week or day of the Kalender. It becomes a planned block of the default length (Einstellungen → Kalender → „Standardlänge“, 60 minutes unless set) on the nearest quarter hour; a page dragged from the sidebar works too. Blocks are drawn with a dashed outline in the accent color, so a plan never looks like a meeting, and show the task or issue they are for.
- **Move, resize, rename, delete**: drag a block to another time or day, drag its foot to change the length, everything on the 15-minute grid. With the keyboard: the arrow keys move a focused block (up/down a quarter hour, left/right a day), Shift and the arrow keys change its length, Enter opens it, Delete removes it („Rückgängig“ brings it back).
- **The block's detail**: the title to rename, the linked task (with its page), issue or page, the Netzplan/Vorgang it would be booked on (the issue's mapping or the page's `vorgang:`), „Fokussitzung starten“ (the focus dialog opens with the block's goal, length and Vorgang), „Aufgabe erledigen“ and „Löschen“.
- **„Im Kalender planen…“** on every open task (Aufgaben list and task widgets, on hover or with the keyboard) and in the menu of every issue: a small picker with today and tomorrow, the length and the free slots between 8 and 18 o'clock (meetings that block time and other blocks are avoided). The way to plan without dragging.
- **Planned next to booked**: the day header of the week and day view shows the planned hours next to the booked hours („2,00 / 1,50 h“), and the „Diese Woche“ widget shows the planned time of each day as a dashed bar behind the booked one. With „Zeiterfassung verwenden“ off, blocks keep working and only the booked side is gone.
- **Fokusblöcke in Outlook eintragen** (Einstellungen → Kalender, off by default; Windows with Outlook Classic): every block gets an appointment „Fokus: <Titel>“ in the default calendar, shown as busy, with the category „Arcalo“ and no reminder; moving, renaming and deleting a block updates or deletes it. Outlook is never started for it: while Outlook is closed the change waits (the block's detail says so, with „Erneut versuchen“) and is written once Outlook runs. The appointment that comes back with the next calendar sync is shown as the block only, never as a second meeting.
- **„Woche vorschlagen“ uses blocks**: a planned block that is over and was not booked becomes a proposal, with the Vorgang of its issue or of its task's page and high confidence when it is linked. A block a focus session was started from is not proposed again (the session is), and a block taken over is never proposed twice.
- **Morning briefing („Morgen-Briefing“)**: today on one page. Today's meetings from your calendars (those used for booking suggestions and not hidden in the Kalender) with their preparation: the meeting's own note, or else the note of the last meeting of the same series or with the same subject; „Beitreten“ for Teams, Zoom, Webex and other links, and „Notiz anlegen“ for the meeting note. Jira issues that are overdue, due today or blocked (status „Blocked“, „Impediment“, „Blockiert“), when a Jira site is set up. Tasks overdue and due today. The last workday against its target with the hours still unbooked, and the way to „Woche vorschlagen“ and the Zeiterfassung; public holidays and absence days count as no gap, and the section is left out while „Zeiterfassung verwenden“ is off. Everything is read with one call.
- **„Was ist heute wichtig“**: two to four short points from the assistant, written from the titles, times and counts of the briefing only (never page contents, places or links). It is written once a day, kept for the day and written again with one click. A briefing with private content (a `#privat` task, a private appointment or a private meeting note) is written by the local model only. Without an AI connection the section shows a hint.
- **When it appears**: Einstellungen → Briefing: off (default), open with the first start of a workday, or a desktop notification at a set time (for example 08:30; empty: at the first start). Only on workdays, never on a public holiday or a full absence day, and once a day. The briefing is always available from the ribbon, the command palette, the „Heute“ widget and the tray menu.
- **Sections in your order**: every section can be switched off and moved, in Einstellungen → Briefing or with „Anpassen“ on the briefing itself.
- **Briefing widget** for the start page: the numbers per section, the meeting under way or next, and the first point of „Was ist heute wichtig“.
- **Jira worklogs in the Zeiterfassung**: an entry booked with an issue key shows the key and what happened to its worklog: „in Jira“, „wird gesendet“ or „fehlgeschlagen“ (the reason on hover) with „Erneut an Jira senden“ on the entry and in its menu.
- **Worklogs follow their entries**: changing the duration, start or comment of an entry updates the worklog already posted to Jira (the edit dialog says so); deleting the entry deletes the worklog too, and the confirmation names the issue. A failed update or deletion is retried like a post. Nothing reaches Jira for a site without „Arbeit auch in Jira protokollieren“ or while „Zeiterfassung verwenden“ is off.
- **„Neu transkribieren“** on a voice note's audio (button on the player, or right-click): another run with a model and language of your choice; the new transcript replaces the old one, which stays when the run fails or is cancelled.
- **Unfinished recordings**: a recording cut off by a crash or a forced quit is offered after the next start in the voice bar: „Als Sprachnotiz speichern“ (stored as FLAC, then transcribed, dated when it ended) or discard.

## Fixed

- **Jira priority colors** for every language and scheme: priorities are colored by Jira's priority id, its default icon or the instance's order, so German names (Höchste, Hoch, Mittel, Niedrig, Niedrigste) and renamed or custom Server priorities are colored too; the „Fristen“ widget and grouping by priority use the same levels.
- **„Nächster Termin“ widget**: at a small size or in a narrow window the meeting title stays visible; place and time are shortened instead.
- **Morning briefing with Arcalo running overnight**: the check of the first start of the day also runs when the date changes while Arcalo runs (in the tray) and the window comes to the front, with the same rules (once a day, workdays only, the notification time).
- **Free slots for focus blocks** only count your own calendars: a colleague who is out of office, or their meetings in a shared calendar, no longer leave the day without a free slot.
- **Focus blocks in Outlook**: deleting a block always removes its appointment, also when Outlook was closed and a new block was created meanwhile, or when the block was deleted while it was still being written. An Outlook that answered too late no longer gets a second appointment, and a block whose appointment you deleted in Outlook is written to the calendar again.
- **„Termine buchen“ in the timesheet** only offers meetings of calendars set to „Für Buchungsvorschläge verwenden“ (no colleagues' meetings), and private appointments are left out in English workspaces too.
- **Morning briefing**: the time section and the „Ungebuchte Stunden“ card skip holidays and full absence days when they look back to the last workday, and per-weekday hours count the same way everywhere. The card's text is shorter („gestern offen“, „am 01.10. offen“) and four cards fit as 2 × 2 in narrow windows.
- **Jira**: a saved search that fails once keeps its issues in its widget until the next good sync. Switching „Arbeitszeit in Jira buchen“ off drops the worklogs still waiting, so old bookings are not posted when you switch it on again.
- **Vacation widget**: the next holiday no longer reads „Morgen · morgen“.
- **Daily review**: meetings still to come show as „steht an“ instead of „alles erledigt“.
- **English workspaces**: quick captures go to an „Inbox“ page instead of „Posteingang“ (an existing inbox page keeps its name).
- **Voice notes**: the audio file uses the same date as the page („Sprachnotiz 02.10.2026 05-31.flac“), and a chosen model that is not downloaded yet shows „Nicht geladen“ instead of „In Verwendung“.
- **Small windows**: below 1000 px the side panel closes by itself so the view keeps its room (Ctrl+J opens it); it comes back when the window gets wide again.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

1. Focus blocks in Outlook: switch on Einstellungen → Kalender → „Fokusblöcke in Outlook eintragen“ with Outlook Classic running, drag a task into tomorrow 10:00: within seconds Outlook's calendar shows „Fokus: <Aufgabe>“ 10:00–11:00 as busy, with the category „Arcalo“ and no reminder; the block's detail says „In Outlook eingetragen“.
2. Move the block to 14:00 and make it 90 minutes long in Arcalo: the same appointment in Outlook moves and grows (no second appointment); rename it in the detail: the subject follows.
3. Ask a colleague to look at your calendar (or use the scheduling assistant of a new meeting): the block's time shows as busy.
4. Sync the Kalender („Jetzt synchronisieren“): the appointment is not shown a second time as a meeting next to the block, also after restarting Arcalo.
5. Close Outlook, move the block: Arcalo does not start Outlook, the detail shows „Wartet auf Outlook“; start Outlook and wait a minute (or click „Erneut versuchen“): the appointment moves.
6. Delete the block in Arcalo: the appointment disappears from Outlook. Delete another block's appointment in Outlook first and then move the block in Arcalo: the appointment is created again.
7. With the new Outlook (olk) only: the setting writes nothing, the block's detail explains why, Arcalo's blocks keep working.
8. Einstellungen → Briefing → „Beim ersten Start“: close Arcalo (also from the tray), start it again on a workday: the briefing opens once; a second start the same day does not open it again. Enter an absence for today in the Kalender and check that the next day's first start still works.
9. „Benachrichtigung“ with a time two minutes ahead: the Windows notification „Morgen-Briefing“ appears once with the counts; with Arcalo in the background, clicking into Arcalo opens the briefing. Inside the quiet hours it waits until they end.
10. Tray menu: „Morgen-Briefing“ shows the window and opens the briefing.
11. With Outlook Classic calendars: only your own calendars (and those marked „Für Buchungsvorschläge verwenden“) appear; a calendar hidden in the Kalender is left out. „Beitreten“ opens Teams.
12. A `#privat` task due today with a cloud provider and a local one (Ollama): „Neu schreiben“ shows „lokal“ next to the model.
13. Briefing „Beim ersten Start“ with Arcalo left in the tray overnight: the next morning, bring the window to the front: the briefing opens once.
14. Jira site with „Arbeit auch in Jira protokollieren“: book `/zeit 1h PROJ-1 …`, change the duration in the Zeiterfassung, then delete the entry: the worklog in Jira changes, then disappears.
15. Voice note: end Arcalo with the Task Manager while recording; on the next start „Unfertige Aufnahme“ appears; „Als Sprachnotiz speichern“ writes the note with its transcript. „Neu transkribieren“ with Large v3 Turbo replaces the transcript.
