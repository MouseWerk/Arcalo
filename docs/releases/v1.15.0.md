Arcalo 1.15.0

## Improved

- Wochenrückblick: a week (Monday to Sunday) on one page with previous/next week and „Diese Woche“: booked hours against the target per day and for the week (targets per weekday, public holidays and absences count), unbooked gaps, the top Netzpläne and Vorgänge, meetings of the synced calendars by day with their booking state, focus blocks, tasks done (repeating ones once per occurrence) and still open or overdue, and the pages worked on with their number of edits. Opens from the command palette, the Kalender's week header, the Tagesrückblick („Woche ansehen“) and the new start page widget „Diese Woche“. Without time tracking the time part is left out.
- Wochenrückblick: „Zusammenfassen“ writes a short summary through the AI routing; a week with a #privat page or a private meeting goes to the local model only. Without an AI provider the button explains how to set one up.
- „Als Wochenbericht speichern“ writes the page „Wochenbericht KW 41 2026“ into the year and month folders (the filing rules apply) with tables for the time, lists of tasks and meetings and links to the pages. Saving the week again updates only the generated part; your notes in the report stay. The template „Wochenbericht“ in „Vorlagen“ (created from the view) shapes new reports.
- Suche nach Bedeutung: the search also finds pages without the exact words. „Angebot Müller“ finds a page about a „Kostenvoranschlag für Kunde Müller“. Such pages are labelled „ähnlich“ with the passage that matched, after the exact hits, in the search sidebar, the command palette and the quick search. „Nur exakt“ next to the results hides them.
- Settings → Suche turns it on or off, shows the embedding model and whether it runs locally, how far the index is, and offers „Index neu aufbauen“. It is on by itself when the embedding model of Settings → KI & Modelle runs locally (Ollama). The index is built in the background, picks up every saved page, continues after a restart and does not slow down typing.
- An embedding model at a provider that is not local is only used when you turn the switch on: pages tagged #privat and searches with a privacy marker never go there, and with „Nur lokal“ it is not used at all. Without an embedding model, or while it cannot be reached, the search finds exact words as before.
- „Ohne KI“: one switch „KI verwenden“ at the top of Settings → KI & Modelle, also offered in the setup as „Mit KI“ or „Ohne KI“. Off, Arcalo shows no AI anywhere: no assistant and no chat, no AI in the editor or the slash menu, no „Zusammenfassen“ in the reviews, the briefing, meeting notes, status reports and voice notes, no AI widgets or commands, no „KI einrichten“ hints and no search by meaning. Nothing is sent to an AI or embedding provider. Everything else works as before; voice notes are still transcribed on this computer.
- Switching „KI verwenden“ back on brings the providers, models and settings back exactly as they were. Existing installations keep AI on after the update.
- Organizations can switch AI off for everyone with the policy `AllowAi = 0` (registry, configuration profile or policy.json); the switch then shows „Von deiner Organisation ausgeschaltet“.
- A new intro: five short scenes tell what Arcalo is, your whole workday in one app: notes and tasks, meetings, time tracking if you book time, your data on your computer and AI only if you want it. Skippable with Esc, steered with the arrow keys and Space, as still slides with reduced motion.
- A shorter setup: language, theme, „Mit KI / Ohne KI“, „Buchst du deine Zeit?“, calendar (optional) and how to start (sample content, empty or an Obsidian vault). Backups, Git sync, security and the desktop keep their defaults and are linked from the last step.
- After the setup the start page greets you with „Erste Schritte“: three or four next steps (first note, today's note, a task, the calendar or your first booking) that tick themselves off and can be hidden. The welcome choice is not asked a second time.
- A new workspace no longer shows the days before its first start as missing hours in the time tracking.
- The startup animation is shorter and uses the system font for the name; the tasks view explains its syntax in two calm lines; the sample „Willkommen“ page tells the new story.
- The last traces of the old name Annalo are gone: the program file is now `arcalo.exe` (Linux: `arcalo`, the old name keeps working as a link), and Arcalo keeps its data under the new app identifier `de.mousewerk.arcalo`. The first start after the update takes over everything by itself: notes, settings, backups, the window and panel layout, saved keys and tokens (also the key of an encrypted workspace) and a data folder you chose. The previous folders and saved credentials stay untouched next to them, so going back to the previous version still works.
- Exported settings, start page boards, themes and links to e-mails from earlier versions still open; the environment variables `ANNALO_…` keep working next to the new `ARCALO_…`.
- macOS asks once more for the microphone and notifications (the app has a new identifier); administrators re-issue configuration profiles for the domain `de.mousewerk.arcalo` (profiles for the old domain still apply meanwhile).

## Fixed

- After going back to 1.14 (or after a rollback of the update) and updating again, the work done in the older version in the meantime is taken over; the state from before is kept in a folder of its own and named in a notice.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Wochenrückblick: with bookings, meetings from the Outlook calendar and a public holiday or vacation day in the week, open it from the Kalender's week header; the day cards show the holiday or absence with no missing hours, the week total matches the Zeiterfassung, and ← / → / „Diese Woche“ move between weeks, also across a DST change.
- „Als Wochenbericht speichern“: the page appears in the year/month folder of the week, its time table, tasks, meetings and page links read correctly in the editor and in a Markdown export; saving again asks, updates the generated part only and keeps a note written in the report.
- With Ollama running and `nomic-embed-text` chosen under Settings → KI & Modelle → Embeddings, open Settings → Suche: the switch „Suche nach Bedeutung“ is on, the index counts up to „Aktuell“; search „Angebot Müller“ (or two words of a page in other words) in the sidebar and in Ctrl+K: pages found by meaning show „ähnlich“ and their passage, „Nur exakt“ hides them, and typing in a large page stays smooth while the index runs.
- Quit Ollama and search again: exact hits appear without delay and Settings → Suche says the model cannot be reached; start Ollama again: the index continues where it stopped.
- Fresh install (no data folder): the startup animation, the intro (Esc skips, arrows move) and the setup in German at 1280 px and in a small window; choose „Ohne KI“ and „Nein“ for time tracking: the start page shows „Erste Schritte“, and no assistant, chat, „KI einrichten“ or AI button appears in the ribbon, the editor (Ctrl+J, slash menu), Ctrl+K or the status bar.
- With „Ohne KI“ and a LiteLLM or Ollama provider set up before, use the app for a while (notes, search, Tagesrückblick, Wochenrückblick): the provider's log or Ollama's console shows no request. Switch „KI verwenden“ on again: the providers are unchanged and the assistant answers.
- Put `"AllowAi": 0` into `policy.json` next to Arcalo.exe (or the registry value `AllowAi` = 0 under HKLM\Software\Policies\MouseWerk\Arcalo) and restart: AI is gone everywhere and Settings → KI & Modelle shows the switch locked with „Von deiner Organisation ausgeschaltet“.
- Update an installed 1.14.1 to 1.15 through Arcalo's own update (Settings → Über): the setup closes the old app, Arcalo starts again by itself, notes, settings, the layout (sidebar width, open panels), the Git token, AI keys and Jira tokens are all there, and an encrypted workspace opens without asking for the recovery key. The program folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Arcalo`, or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Annalo` for installs from 1.6) holds `arcalo.exe` and no `annalo.exe`; `%APPDATA%\de.mousewerk.arcalo` holds the data and `%APPDATA%\app.annalo.desktop` is still there unchanged.
- Pinned taskbar shortcut: pinned before the update, it still starts Arcalo afterwards and the running app groups under the same icon, not a second one. If Windows shows a second icon, unpin and pin again once and note it in the findings.
- Autostart: switched on before the update (Settings → Desktop), log off and on: Arcalo starts minimized and Task Manager → Autostart shows „Arcalo“ with `arcalo.exe`. Switched off in Task Manager before the update, it stays off.
- Apps & features: one entry „Arcalo“ with the new version. Uninstall it with „App-Daten löschen“: `%APPDATA%` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%` hold neither `de.mousewerk.arcalo` nor `app.annalo.desktop` any more, the Start menu and desktop shortcuts are gone.
- Credential Manager → Windows Credentials: entries „Arcalo“ exist for the saved keys and tokens next to the old „Annalo“ ones. Delete an AI key in Arcalo and restart: it stays deleted.
- Start menu and desktop shortcuts start `arcalo.exe`; a notification of Arcalo (e.g. the end-of-day reminder) shows the Arcalo name and icon and its buttons work.
- Microsoft Store build (MSIX) on a computer that had the installer version 1.14: the Store app opens the same notes, settings and credentials; on Windows 11 `%APPDATA%\de.mousewerk.arcalo` is the real folder (not below `Packages\…\LocalCache`).
- Portable ZIP unpacked over a portable folder of 1.14: the data, the layout and the saved keys of that copy are there; a second start of the same copy is refused while it runs.
