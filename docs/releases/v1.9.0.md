Arcalo 1.9.0

## New

- **Automatic filing („Ordner & Ablage“)**: every kind of page Arcalo creates goes into its folder, with subfolders by date: daily notes into `Journal/2026/10 – Oktober`, meeting notes into `Besprechungen/2026/10 – Oktober` (or by series: `Besprechungen/Jour fixe`), voice notes into `Sprachnotizen/2026/10 – Oktober`, mail notes into `E-Mails/2026/10 – Oktober` (below the parent chosen in the mail dialog), Jira issue notes by project into `Jira/ABC Portal/ABC-12 Login`, imported bookmarks into `Lesezeichen` and a new inbox page at the top level. Month folders start with their number, so they sort by name, and are named in the display language („10 – October“); a folder made in one language is found again in the other.
- **Existing folders are used**: a top-level „Besprechungen“, „Meetings“, „Journal“ or „Sprachnotizen“ you already have becomes the root of its kind; nothing is renamed, and existing pages are never moved by the update.
- **Einstellungen → Ordner & Ablage**: per kind the folder (a path like `Arbeit/Journal` works) and the subfolders: none, year, year and month, year and calendar week („KW 40“), and for meetings „Nach Serie“. Each row shows an example path for today.
- **Rules**: „Hat Tag #kunde-x → Kunden/X“, „Eigenschaft status = Offen → …“, „Jira-Projekt ABC → …“, „Netzplan NP-1 → …“ and „Titel beginnt mit … → …“, in an ordered list; the first matching rule decides the folder of a new page, before its kind. „Seite testen“ shows where a page would go with the rules on screen, before saving.
- **„Aufräumen…“** (command palette, tree options, and the context menu of every folder for just its pages): a dry run of the moves that would bring existing pages into the folders of their kind and the rules, from → to, with the folders that would be created and the deciding rule; tick the ones you want and apply them at once. The toast and „Letztes Aufräumen rückgängig machen“ put them back and remove the folders the tidy-up created. A page you placed in a folder of your own stays there unless a rule names it.
- **Sorting per folder**: manual, name, last modified or created, and „Ordner zuerst“, from the folder's context menu (the top level from the tree options); remembered per folder.
- **Folder colors**: a color for a page's icon from the theme colors (accent, blue, green, amber, red, violet, gray), readable in light and dark; the icon itself from the existing set.
- **Smart folders („Intelligente Ordner“)** above the tree: Zuletzt bearbeitet, Favoriten, Ohne Ordner, Verwaiste Seiten (no links in or out), nach Tag, nach Jira-Projekt and nach Netzplan, each with its count and expanding to its pages; read-only lists computed in the database. The section can be collapsed and hidden.
- **Multi-select and „Verschieben nach…“**: Shift-click selects a range, Ctrl-click (once a selection exists) adds or removes a page, Shift and the arrow keys extend it, Escape clears it. Drag the selection onto a folder, or use „Verschieben nach…“: a fuzzy folder picker (also for a single page, from its context menu or the command palette) that moves all pages at once, with „Rückgängig“.
- **Tree filter**: a field above the tree filters it by title (accents ignored) and keeps the folders around each hit open.
- **Page embeds (Obsidian syntax)**: `![[Seite]]` shows a whole page, `![[Seite#Überschrift]]` the section up to the next heading of the same or a higher level, `![[Seite#^block]]` the one block marked `^block` (at the end of a paragraph, list item or heading, or alone on the line after a block). Read-only and live: an embed follows edits of its page in another pane or tab, in a quiet frame with the source's title as a link and a chevron to collapse it. Embeds nest up to three levels; a cycle (A embeds B embeds A) and a missing page or section show a calm notice, a missing page with „Seite anlegen“. Files keep their own embeds (`![[bild.png]]`, `![[Angebot.pdf]]`); the Markdown stays exactly `![[…]]` in the mirror, Git sync and Obsidian import and export. An embed counts as a link (backlinks). `![[` completes pages, then the page's headings after `#` and its block ids after `#^` (the same works for `[[Seite#`). Slash command „Seite einbetten“.
- **Queries in notes**: a ```` ```query ```` block runs the dashboard's „Abfrage“ live in the note: sources tasks, pages, bookings and meetings, the same filter line in German and English (`#projekt status: offen fällig: woche`), and option lines first: `from:`/`aus:`, `show:`/`anzeige:` (`table`, `list`, `count`, `chart`), `sort:` (`title`, `due`, `prio`, `-` or `desc` for descending), `limit:`, `group:`, `columns:`. Tasks in the result can be ticked; the result refreshes on edits, syncs and bookings. Under it: the number of hits, what was not understood, and „Abfrage bearbeiten“, which shows the source. With time tracking off, bookings are not shown. Slash command „Abfrage“.
- **Mermaid diagrams**: a ```` ```mermaid ```` block renders as a diagram: flowchart, sequence, Gantt, class, state, ER and mind map. The library is part of the app and loads with the first diagram only (never from the internet), under the app's content security policy. Diagrams follow the light and dark theme; errors show the message with the source; „Quelltext“ shows the code while you edit, the preview updates as you type; export as SVG or PNG. Slash command „Diagramm (Mermaid)“.
- **Everywhere a note goes**: HTML share renders embeds in frames, diagrams as inline SVG and queries as static tables; print/PDF renders every embed, diagram and query first (diagrams light) and expands collapsed embeds; presentation mode shows embedded sections and diagrams on the slides.
- **Lazy rendering**: embeds, diagrams and queries render when they scroll into view, so a page with ten embeds, three queries and three diagrams opens as fast as one without.
- **Updates in the background**: Arcalo checks at start and every few hours (Settings → Über → Updates: 1 hour to a week, default 6), downloads a new version quietly with its progress in the status bar (and the tray tooltip), pausable, and resumes an interrupted download where it stopped. Every file is checked against the built-in signature key. Then a calm hint in the status bar, „Update 1.9.1 bereit – wird beim Beenden von Arcalo installiert“, with „Jetzt neu starten“ and „Später“; no dialog. Quitting Arcalo (tray „Beenden“, ⌘Q, closing the window; on macOS and Linux also logging out) installs it: on Windows the installer runs in passive update mode after the workspace was saved and closed, macOS replaces the app, the AppImage replaces itself; a .deb only shows the notice. The mode „Nur benachrichtigen“ keeps the click-to-install flow of before.
- **„Jetzt neu starten“ keeps the session**: the same tabs, panes and active page open again (Settings → Über → „Sitzung nach „Jetzt neu starten“ wiederherstellen“; the cursor position is not restored).
- **„Neu in Arcalo“**: after an update a short dialog shows the highlights of the new version (once), each with a way to try it; Settings → Über → „Neu in Arcalo“ lists every version with its highlights and the full release notes (bundled, or loaded from GitHub for versions the app does not carry).
- **„Diese Version überspringen“ and „Später erinnern“** (morgen, nächste Woche) on the update hint; Settings → Über shows a skipped version with „Rückgängig“.
- **Way back when an update does not start**: before installing, the database is backed up as `arcalo-pre-update-<alt>-<neu>.db` in the backup folder and the running version is kept (Windows: the program folder; macOS: the app as .tar.gz; Linux: the AppImage). If the new version fails to start twice in a row, the next start asks in a small native dialog whether to return; „Ja“ restores the database and the previous version and never offers the failed version again. Settings → Über offers „Zu Version … zurückkehren“ by hand while the copy exists, with a warning that changes since the update are lost. A version that cannot even start its own startup code is beyond this (docs/admin/updates.md).
- **Update source and policies for organizations**: an own update source (URL of a `latest.json` on an internal server, or a network folder like `\\server\freigabe\arcalo`) with GitHub as fallback; proxy and CA of Settings → Netzwerk apply. Policies from the registry (`HKLM\Software\Policies\MouseWerk\Arcalo` over HKCU), macOS managed preferences or a `policy.json` next to the program or in the system folder: `UpdateMode` (auto, notify, off), `UpdateUrl`, `AllowGitHubFallback`, `PinnedVersion`, `InstallWindow` („18:00-07:00“), `CheckIntervalHours`. Settings shows „Von Ihrer Organisation verwaltet“ and locks those fields. `scripts/mirror-release.py` mirrors a release to a share. All in `docs/admin/updates.md`.

- **Graph view („Graph“)**: every page as a node, `[[links]]` and `![[embeds]]` as edges, from the ribbon, the command palette („Graphansicht öffnen“) and the quick search. Pan and zoom with the mouse, wheel, trackpad and keys, drag nodes, hover lights up a page's neighbors and dims the rest with its title; a click opens the page, Ctrl/Cmd+click in a new tab; labels appear as you zoom in (big pages first); „Knoten suchen“ highlights the hits and Enter flies to the next; „An Bildschirm anpassen“. Drawn on a canvas, the force layout runs in the background (Web Worker) and unfolds as it settles; the layout is stored per workspace, so reopening shows the same picture at once. 5,000 pages with 20,000 links stay smooth.
- **Filters**: tag, folder (with its subfolders), project (Netzplan), Jira project, date range (created or modified), orphans on/off, unresolved links as hollow ghost nodes, daily notes on/off, tags and attachments as nodes, and the depth (1–5 links) from a node you searched for. The page filters run in the database; a save, rename, move or delete updates the graph in place.
- **Color groups and display**: rules by tag, folder or query (`tag:kunde path:Projekte`, `jira:ABC`, `netzplan:NP-1`) → a color of the theme's chart palette, readable in light and dark; without rules each top-level folder gets its color (a folder holding the whole workspace is split by its subfolders), shown in a legend. Node size by links, line thickness, the forces (center, repulsion, link distance) and animation (off with reduced motion). „Voreinstellungen“ save filters and groups per workspace.
- **Local graph** in the side panel (tab „Graph“ next to outline and links): the active page and its neighbors 1–3 links away, with the same interactions; it follows the page you open and keeps the nodes both pages share in place.
- **Accessible**: the arrow keys move from node to node (announced with title, links and folder), Enter opens, Shift+arrows pan, + and − zoom, 0 fits, Home picks the most linked page; „Als Liste anzeigen“ lists the filtered pages with folder and links.
- **PNG export** of the current view at twice its size on the theme's background, for presentations.

- **Canvas**: a new kind of page, an infinite board of cards and connections, stored in the open JSON Canvas format (jsoncanvas.org) as Obsidian uses it, exactly as written: fields Arcalo does not know stay as they are. „Neue Canvas“ in the tree (toolbar and „Neu“ in a page's context menu), the command palette and the slash menu („/canvas“ creates one below the note and links it); new canvases go flat into the folder „Canvas“ (Settings → Ordner & Ablage, type „Canvas“).
- **Cards**: text cards in Markdown (double-click or Enter to write, rendered like notes, `[[links]]` and diagrams included); note cards that show a page live and read-only with its own scrolling (double-click or the arrow opens it); images and files; web links with the page's title; Jira issues with key, status, summary and assignee (issues outside the cache are fetched for their card); groups as labelled frames that take their cards along when moved. Cards and connections take the six JSON Canvas colors, mapped to calm colors for light and dark.
- **Editing**: pan with the wheel, the middle button or Space+drag, zoom with Ctrl+wheel or a trackpad pinch around the pointer, „Alles zeigen“ (Shift+1) and a minimap; snap to the grid (on by default, Alt drags freely); box selection and Shift-click; drag, resize from the corners, align and distribute; copy, cut, paste (also between canvases and to and from Obsidian) and duplicate (Ctrl+D); arrow keys move, Delete removes, Ctrl+A selects all, Escape leaves; undo and redo for every change. Connections are drawn from the round handles on a card's sides, onto another card or onto the empty board (a new card there); they get an arrow, a label, a color and a curved or straight line.
- **Drop onto a canvas**: pages from the tree (note cards), issues from the Issues page, files from Explorer/Finder (stored as attachments) and links.
- **Everywhere a page goes**: the Markdown mirror and the Git sync write `<Titel>.canvas`, vault import reads `.canvas` files and export writes them (note cards pointing to where the pages are), backups contain it, note cards and links in text cards count as links (backlinks, renames follow into the canvas, the graph), search finds the cards' text. Export as PNG and SVG, and „Als HTML-Datei teilen“ as a static picture of the board.
- **Fast with large boards**: only the cards near the visible part are rendered, panning moves the board without redrawing it, and far out cards show just their first line; 300 cards stay smooth.

## Fixed

- **Links survive moves**: links go by page title, so moving pages (one, many, or by the tidy-up) never breaks `[[links]]` or backlinks; the Markdown mirror writes a moved page at its new path and removes the old file, and the Git sync records it as a rename of the same content instead of an edit.
- **Morning briefing** finds the last note of a meeting subject also when meeting notes are in month or series folders.
- **The Journal starts collapsed** also when today's note sits in a month folder.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Fresh install: create today's daily note, a meeting note from the Kalender, a voice note and a Jira issue note: they are in `Journal/…/10 – Oktober`, `Besprechungen/…`, `Sprachnotizen/…` and `Jira/<Projekt>`.
- Update from 1.8 with an existing „Besprechungen“ folder full of notes: nothing moved after the start; the next meeting note goes into `Besprechungen/2026/<Monat>`.
- „Aufräumen…“ on a large workspace: the preview opens within a second or two, applying and „Rückgängig“ restore the tree exactly.
- Markdown mirror and Git sync after a tidy-up: the files moved in the mirror folder, `git log --stat -M` shows renames.
- Multi-select with Shift and Ctrl-click, drag onto a folder, „Verschieben nach…“ with the keyboard (arrows, Enter).
- Switch the display language to English: new month folders are named in English and the German ones are still used.
- Tree filter, smart folders, sort and color menus in the light and dark theme at 900 px window width and in a split pane.
- Embeds, queries and Mermaid: a page with `![[Seite#Überschrift]]`, `![[Seite#^id]]`, a ```` ```query ```` with ticks and seven diagram types renders in light and dark, prints to PDF with all of them (diagrams light), shares as HTML and presents; export a diagram as PNG and open it.
- Install on quit: with 1.9.0 installed per user, publish 1.9.1 to a test feed; leave Arcalo running. The status bar shows the download (pause and continue once; pull the network cable midway and plug it in again: the download continues, it does not start over), then „Update 1.9.1 bereit – wird beim Beenden von Arcalo installiert“. Type a sentence and quit via the tray „Beenden“: the installer runs passively without Arcalo restarting, no dead tray icon. Start Arcalo: „auf Version 1.9.1 aktualisiert“, „Neu in Arcalo“ once, the sentence is there, `arcalo-pre-update-1.9.0-1.9.1.db` in the backup folder and `rollback\1.9.0` in the data folder. Repeat with „Jetzt neu starten“: Arcalo comes back with the same tabs. Also: shut Windows down with an update ready: nothing breaks, the update installs at the next quit.
- Rollback after a forced failed start: after an update to 1.9.1, make the new version fail at start twice (e.g. a test build that exits in setup, or kill it before its window appears twice in a row). The third start shows „Arcalo 1.9.1 konnte zweimal nicht starten – zu 1.9.0 zurückkehren?“; „Ja“ starts 1.9.0 with the database of before the update and the toast „Zurück auf Version 1.9.0“; 1.9.1 is not offered again. Also the manual „Zu 1.9.0 zurückkehren“ in Settings → Über with its warning.
- HKLM policy with an internal share: import the `.reg` from docs/admin/updates.md (UpdateUrl `\\server\share\arcalo`, AllowGitHubFallback 0, InstallWindow around now) and mirror 1.9.1 there with `scripts/mirror-release.py`. Settings → Über shows „Von Ihrer Organisation verwaltet“ with the fields locked; the update comes from the share (log: source), GitHub is not contacted (firewall or proxy log); an HKCU value that differs from HKLM loses; with the share unreachable and AllowGitHubFallback 1 GitHub is used. A tampered file on the share is refused. Outside the install window the update waits and „Jetzt neu starten“ is not offered.
- Skip and remind: „Diese Version überspringen“ on the hint: no hint after a restart and a new check, Settings → Über shows it with „Rückgängig“; a newer version is offered again. „Morgen erinnern“: no hint and no install on quit until tomorrow; „Jetzt anzeigen“ brings it back.
- Graph view on a large workspace (thousands of pages): opens within two seconds, stays responsive while the layout settles, reopens instantly; light and dark, at 900 px and in a split pane; PNG export opens in an image viewer; the local graph follows the active page.
