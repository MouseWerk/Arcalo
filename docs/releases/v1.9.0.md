Arcalo 1.9.0

## New

- **Automatic filing („Ordner & Ablage“)**: every kind of page Arcalo creates goes into its folder, with subfolders by date: daily notes into `Journal/2026/10 – Oktober`, meeting notes into `Besprechungen/2026/10 – Oktober` (or by series: `Besprechungen/Jour fixe`), voice notes into `Sprachnotizen/2026/10 – Oktober`, mail notes into `E-Mails/2026/10 – Oktober` (below the parent chosen in the mail dialog), Jira issue notes by project into `Jira/ABC Portal/ABC-12 Login`, imported bookmarks into `Lesezeichen` and a new inbox page at the top level. Month folders start with their number, so they sort by name, and are named in the display language („10 – October“); a folder made in one language is found again in the other.
- **Existing folders are used**: a top-level „Besprechungen“, „Meetings“, „Journal“ or „Sprachnotizen“ you already have becomes the root of its kind; nothing is renamed, and existing pages are never moved by the update.
- **Einstellungen → Ordner & Ablage**: per kind the folder (a path like `Arbeit/Journal` works) and the subfolders: none, year, year and month, year and calendar week („KW 40“), and for meetings „Nach Serie“. Each row shows an example path for today.
- **Rules**: „Hat Tag #kunde-x → Kunden/X“, „Eigenschaft status = Offen → …“, „Jira-Projekt ABC → …“, „Netzplan NP-1 → …“ and „Titel beginnt mit … → …“, in an ordered list; the first matching rule decides the folder of a new page, before its kind. „Seite testen“ shows where a page would go with the rules on screen, before saving.
- **„Aufräumen…“** (command palette, tree options, and the context menu of every folder for just its pages): a dry run of the moves that would bring existing pages into the folders of their kind and the rules, from → to, with the folders that would be created and the deciding rule; tick the ones you want and apply them at once. The toast and „Letztes Aufräumen rückgängig machen“ put them back and remove the folders the tidy-up created. A page you placed in a folder of your own stays there unless a rule names it.
- **Sorting per folder**: manual, name, last modified or created, and „Ordner zuerst“, from the folder's context menu (the top level from the tree options); remembered per folder.
- **Folder colors**: a color for a page's icon from the theme colors (accent, blue, green, amber, red, violet, gray), readable in light and dark; the icon itself from the existing set.
- **Smart folders („Intelligente Ordner“)** above the tree: Zuletzt bearbeitet, Favoriten, Ohne Ordner, Verwaiste Seiten (no links in or out), nach Tag, nach Jira-Projekt and nach Netzplan, each with its count and expanding to its pages; read-only lists computed in the database. The section can be collapsed and hidden.
- **Multi-select and „Verschieben nach…“**: Shift-click selects a range, Ctrl-click (once a selection exists) adds or removes a page, Shift and the arrow keys extend it, Escape clears it. Drag the selection onto a folder, or use „Verschieben nach…“: a fuzzy folder picker (also for a single page, from its context menu or the command palette) that moves all pages at once, with „Rückgängig“.
- **Tree filter**: a field above the tree filters it by title (accents ignored) and keeps the folders around each hit open.

## Fixed

- **Links survive moves**: links go by page title, so moving pages (one, many, or by the tidy-up) never breaks `[[links]]` or backlinks; the Markdown mirror writes a moved page at its new path and removes the old file, and the Git sync records it as a rename of the same content instead of an edit.
- **Morning briefing** finds the last note of a meeting subject also when meeting notes are in month or series folders.
- **The Journal starts collapsed** also when today's note sits in a month folder.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Fresh install: create today's daily note, a meeting note from the Kalender, a voice note and a Jira issue note: they are in `Journal/…/10 – Oktober`, `Besprechungen/…`, `Sprachnotizen/…` and `Jira/<Projekt>`.
- Update from 1.8 with an existing „Besprechungen“ folder full of notes: nothing moved after the start; the next meeting note goes into `Besprechungen/2026/<Monat>`.
- „Aufräumen…“ on a large workspace: the preview opens within a second or two, applying and „Rückgängig“ restore the tree exactly.
- Markdown mirror and Git sync after a tidy-up: the files moved in the mirror folder, `git log --stat -M` shows renames.
- Multi-select with Shift and Ctrl-click, drag onto a folder, „Verschieben nach…“ with the keyboard (arrows, Enter).
- Switch the display language to English: new month folders are named in English and the German ones are still used.
- Tree filter, smart folders, sort and color menus in the light and dark theme at 900 px window width and in a split pane.
