Arcalo 1.21.0

## Improved

- Windows with Outlook (classic): create appointments in the Kalender by dragging over a free stretch, a double click, N or „Neuer Termin…“; they go to your Outlook calendar.
- Move, resize, rename and delete your own appointments in the Kalender; only what you change is written to Outlook, and a delete can be undone with „Rückgängig“.
- Meetings you organize ask „Aktualisierung an … Teilnehmende senden?“ before Outlook sends the change; without „Senden und speichern“ nothing changes. Meetings from others and series stay as they are, with „In Outlook öffnen“.
- When Outlook is closed, a change shows „Wartet auf Outlook“ and is written once Outlook runs; Arcalo never starts Outlook for it.
- If the same detail was changed in Outlook meanwhile, the appointment shows both and you choose „Outlook-Version übernehmen“ or „Meine Änderung behalten“.
- Settings → Kalender → „Termine anlegen“: „Termine in Outlook eintragen“ (off until you switch it on; Arcalo offers it the first time you create an appointment), the calendar for new appointments and their length.

## Fixed

- Narrow appointments in the week view end their title with „…“ instead of breaking it in the middle of a word.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- With Outlook (classic) running: double-click a free slot in the Kalender, switch on writing in the offer, create the appointment. It appears in Outlook once, without a category, and no second copy appears after a sync.
- Create one by dragging over a free stretch. Move one by dragging and with the arrow keys, resize it, rename it, change its place: Outlook shows each change, and its text, reminder and categories stay as they were.
- Delete an appointment: it is in Outlook's „Gelöschte Elemente“. „Rückgängig“: it is back in the calendar.
- Close Outlook and change an appointment: „Wartet auf Outlook“, Outlook does not start. Open Outlook and wait a minute (or „Erneut versuchen“): the change is written.
- Change the title in Outlook and in Arcalo before a sync: both versions are shown; try both choices. Change different details on each side: no question.
- A meeting you organize with attendees: moving it asks to send the update; „Abbrechen“ leaves Outlook unchanged; „Senden und speichern“ sends it (check an attendee's inbox). A meeting with only yourself invited asks nothing.
- A meeting from a colleague and an appointment of a series cannot be moved; „In Outlook öffnen“ opens them.
- „Kalender für neue Termine“ set to another own calendar: new appointments land there.
- A private appointment can be moved without its subject changing in Outlook.
- With the new Outlook or without Outlook: „Neuer Termin“ is not offered and Settings → Kalender says why.
