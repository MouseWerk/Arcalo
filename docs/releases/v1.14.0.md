Arcalo 1.14.0

## Improved

- A home button at the left of each tab bar shows the start page: it switches to the start page tab of that pane, or opens one.
- The buttons at the top of the sidebar (Dateien, Suche, Lesezeichen, Tags) have the size of the ribbon's buttons and sit on one line with them and the tab bar.
- A calmer look for selected and current items: pages in the tree, tabs, selected tasks, the picked calendar event, chips, cards and settings now show their state with a quiet gray tint and bolder text instead of colored frames, glows or bars, and the keyboard focus is a neutral gray ring that only appears when you use the keyboard. Colored bars at the side are gone too: callouts show a tint with their icon, calendar colors are a fill or a dot, warnings a tinted row. The high contrast themes keep a clear gray edge on selected rows.
- Help in the app: the „?“ above the settings gear opens the documentation on the website (in the language of the app), the keyboard shortcuts, the release notes, „Feedback geben“ and „Fehler melden“. F1 opens the documentation; the same entries are in the command palette and under Settings → Über.
- „Feedback geben“ and „Fehler melden“ open a form on GitHub with only the Arcalo version and your system filled in, nothing from your notes.
- The settings for AI providers, network, backups, Git sync, encryption, calendars, updates and keyboard link to their page in the documentation („Mehr in der Doku“).
- A chat view besides the assistant in the side panel: „Chat“ in the ribbon, Ctrl+Shift+J or „Chat öffnen“ in the command palette opens a chatbot as a tab, with your chats on the left (grouped by Heute, Gestern, Letzte 7 Tage and Älter, pinned ones on top, with search) and the conversation in a calm reading column with a large input box.
- The chat view answers as a plain chatbot without your notes; „Mit meinen Notizen“ in the input box lets the answers search your notes, cite sources and use the assistant's tools. „Seite anhängen“ sends one page along. #privat questions stay on the local model as before.
- The side panel and the chat view share one chat history: renaming, pinning and deleting (with „Rückgängig“) shows in both, a chat opens in the other place with one click, and a chat open in both shows each new answer in both.
- Answers of the assistant read better: code blocks with syntax highlighting, the language and a „Kopieren“ button, tables that scroll in their own box, task lists, Mermaid diagrams that can be switched to their source, and links that open in the browser with the address shown on hover. HTML in an answer is shown as text.

## Fixed

- With several pages selected in a narrow sidebar, the bar „2 ausgewählt“ cut off its clear button at the edge.
## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Open two pages in tabs, click the house at the left of the tab bar: the start page opens in a new tab; open a page in that tab and click the house again: no second start page tab, the existing one is shown.
- Look at the top left corner in light and dark: the ribbon's sidebar button and the sidebar's buttons have the same size and sit on one line with the tabs.
- In the light, dark and both high contrast themes: Ctrl+click two pages in the tree, select tasks in Aufgaben, click a meeting in Kalender and open the command palette; the selection is clearly visible without any colored frame, and Tab shows a gray focus ring.
- A note with callouts (`> [!warning]`, `> [!tip]`, `> [!note]`) shows each with its icon and tint, without a bar at the left.
- Click the „?“ above the settings gear: the menu opens to the right with Dokumentation, Tastenkürzel, Versionshinweise, Feedback geben and Fehler melden; „Dokumentation“ and F1 open the German documentation in the default browser (the English one with the app in English).
- „Fehler melden“ and „Feedback geben“ open the GitHub form with the Arcalo version and „Windows 11 …“ (not „Windows 10“ on a Windows 11 computer) already filled in, and nothing else.
- Press Ctrl+Shift+J: the chat view opens as a tab; ask something, press Stop during a long answer, then ask for code: the code block is highlighted and „Kopieren“ puts it on the clipboard (paste it into Notepad). Open the same chat in the side panel (Ctrl+J, Verlauf) and ask there: the answer appears in the chat view too.
- Make the window about 900 px wide with the chat view open: the chat list folds into a button, nothing scrolls sideways and the input box stays fully visible; check light, dark and high contrast.
