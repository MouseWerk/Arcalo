Annalo 1.7.0

## New

- **Overtime balance („Gleitzeitsaldo“)**: a start page widget with your balance as one big number (+12,50 h), its change over the last seven days and a sparkline of the last twelve weeks. Booked hours minus the target of every day since a start date, plus an opening balance; a running timer counts until now, and today counts once its target is reached. Einstellungen → Zeiterfassung → „Saldo und Urlaub“ sets the start date and opening balance, a target per weekday (or the daily target on your workdays, as before), your vacation days and carry-over, and the German state for public holidays.
- **Public holidays of every German state**: computed by Annalo, including the Easter-based ones (Karfreitag, Ostermontag, Christi Himmelfahrt, Pfingstmontag, Fronleichnam), Buß- und Bettag in Sachsen, Reformationstag, Allerheiligen, Heilige Drei Könige, Mariä Himmelfahrt (Bayern, Saarland), Weltkindertag (Thüringen) and Frauentag (Berlin, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern). They have no target and show in the Kalender's day header.
- **Absence days**: vacation, sick, time off in lieu („Gleitzeitausgleich“: the day keeps its target and the balance pays for it) and other absences, as full or half days, for one day or a range (only workdays are entered). Enter them from the balance and vacation widgets or from any day's header in the Kalender; the header shows what is entered and removes it again. The week proposal, its Friday reminder and the Tagesrückblick never count a holiday or an absence day as a gap.
- **Vacation widget**: days left as a ring, taken and planned days, the entitlement of the year, your next absences and the next public holiday with a countdown.
- **Deadlines („Fristen“)**: due dates of open tasks and of date properties in your pages' tables, sorted by date with „in 3 Tagen“ countdowns and overdue items in red; the horizon (7 to 90 days) and the sources can be set. Further sources (Jira due dates) plug in through one provider list.
- **Flagged Outlook e-mails** (Windows, Outlook Classic): the flagged mails of Outlook's To-Do list with sender, subject and the flag's due date; „In Outlook öffnen“ shows the mail, „Aufgabe“ takes it over through the e-mail dialog you know. Outlook is never started for it. On other systems the gallery does not offer the widget.
- **Next meeting**: the meeting running now or next with a live countdown, place, „Beitreten“ for Teams, Zoom, Webex and other links, the meeting note, and the meeting after it.
- **Team availability**: who of the colleagues whose calendars you sync (shared calendars, other mailboxes, also free/busy only) is free, busy, tentative or out of office right now, and until when; pick the people in the widget's settings.
- **Charts**: bars, a line or a donut over a page's table (subpages counted or a number property summed, grouped by any property, select options in their own colors) or over your bookings (hours per Netzplan, Vorgang, Leistungsart, week or month). The charts are drawn by Annalo itself, follow the theme, show values on hover and switch to a table; screen readers get a title and the data table.
- **Activity heatmap**: the last twelve months of notes edited or hours booked per day, GitHub-style, with a tooltip per day.
- **Kanban mini-board**: any page with subpages and a select, person or checkbox property as compact columns on the start page; drag a card to another column (or Alt+arrow keys) to change the property.
- Widgets about hours, the balance and vacation, and charts of the bookings are hidden while „Zeiterfassung verwenden“ is off, like the other time widgets.
- **Widgets in five sizes**: every widget of the start page can be small, medium, wide, tall or wide and tall. In edit mode the size buttons show each size to scale, the corner handle still sets any size, and the keys 1 to 5 pick a size for the focused widget (the arrow keys move it, Shift and the arrow keys resize it). Outside edit mode each widget's menu („…“ next to the gear) has the sizes, its settings, „Duplizieren“ and „Entfernen“. The others make room without overlapping, and the layout is saved at once.
- **Start pages for every purpose**: the tabs of the start page now come with the templates „Sprint“ (tasks of the week, the focus timer, meetings, a checklist and what others changed) and „Persönlich“ (scratchpad, inbox, a year ago, writing and a checklist) next to „Tagesstart“, „Projektleitung“ and „Minimal“. Add, rename (double-click or F2), reorder (Alt and the arrow keys) and delete them as before; each keeps its own layout, and the one used last opens. A start page of 1.6 opens exactly as it was.
- **Start pages as files**: „Board exportieren …“ writes a `.dashboard.json` file that others can import. Settings that hold credentials are never written into it. On import the file is checked, and widgets this version does not know are left out with a notice naming them. Widgets of a newer version stay on your boards (hidden) instead of being lost.
- **Notizzettel**: a scratchpad on the start page, written in Markdown with `- [ ]` checkboxes and saved as you type. It is a page of its own (created with the first words), so it is in every backup and in Git sync; „Ansicht mit Checkboxen“ shows it rendered with checkboxes to tick.
- **Posteingang**: the quick captures waiting on the inbox page (quick capture's target „Posteingang“), newest first with their time. „Ablegen in …“ moves an entry into any page, „Erledigt“ takes it off the inbox; an inbox edited meanwhile is never cut in the wrong place.
- **Vor einem Jahr**: the pages created or edited on this day in earlier years and that day's daily note, plus a random note not changed for a month, the same all day, another one with „Mischen“.
- **Per Git-Sync geändert**: the pages the last syncs took over from others (changed, new, in conflict or moved to the trash), with a hint and the way to the settings while Git sync is off.
- **Schreiben**: words written per day over the last 14 or 30 days, new pages and the days in a row, from the activity journal (one small query, nothing scans your pages).
- **Weitere Zeitzonen in der Uhr**: the clock shows more time zones with their offset and whether it is already tomorrow there, in 12 or 24 hours as your region writes them (or as you choose).
- **Fokus-Timer**: the running focus session as a countdown ring with its goal, „25 Min.“ and „50 Min.“ to start one, the break and „Nächste Sitzung“. It is the same focus session as in the status bar, not a second timer.
- **Checkliste**: a short list to tick off, reorder (Alt and the arrow keys) and clear, kept in the widget.
- **Sicherung & Sync**: the last backup with each destination's state and the last Git sync with its error or waiting changes, with „Jetzt sichern“ and „Jetzt synchronisieren“.
- **Seite einbetten** has an „Öffnen“ button; the Links widget shows your link groups as before.
- **For widget authors**: a widget is one file in `ui/src/components/dashboard/widgets/` with a `defineWidget` call (see docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, „Start page widgets: how to add one“). Its data loads only once it scrolls into view.

## Fixed

- **Number notation**: Einstellungen → Sprache & Format „Zahlen“ (1.234,5 or 1,234.5) was not saved and fell back to the decimal comma. It is kept now, and without a choice numbers follow the display language: English shows 28.00 h, German 28,00 h.
- **Hours in English everywhere**: building on the saved „Zahlen“ choice, what Annalo writes itself follows it too: reminders, the tray, the Tagesrückblick, the week proposal and the Markdown copy show 28.00 h in English and 28,00 h in German (or as chosen), and the remaining places of the interface that always wrote a comma (theme contrast, the assistant's character counts, the focus length) follow it as well. The date formats 24/09/2026 and 09/24/2026 are kept after a restart now. CATS and SAP exports keep the format SAP expects.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

1. Flagged e-mails: flag three mails in Outlook Classic (one with „Heute“, one with „Nächste Woche“, one without a date, one of them in a subfolder); the „Markierte E-Mails“ widget lists all three with their due countdown, the earliest first. With Outlook closed the widget says Outlook is not running and Annalo does not start it.
2. Flagged e-mails: „In Outlook öffnen“ shows the mail in its own Outlook window; „Aufgabe“ opens „E-Mail übernehmen“ with the subject as the task text, and the task links back to the mail.
3. Flagged e-mails with the new Outlook (olk) only: the widget explains that Outlook (classic) is needed.
4. Team availability: select two colleagues' calendars (one shared with details, one as „nur Frei/Gebucht“) in Einstellungen → Kalender; the „Team“ widget shows the one in a meeting as busy with the subject and „bis 11:30“, the free/busy one without a subject, and someone with an out-of-office appointment as away.
5. Team availability: when a meeting ends, the widget changes the state within a minute without reloading.
6. Start page: switch a widget between the five sizes from its menu (outside edit mode) and with the keys 1–5 in edit mode; nothing overlaps, and the sizes are kept after a restart.
7. Export a start page to `Documents\Arbeit.dashboard.json`, import it again: the new tab has the same widgets; open the file in an editor and check that no token or password appears.
8. Quick capture (its global shortcut) with the target „Posteingang“: the entry appears in the inbox widget; „Ablegen in …“ moves it into a page.
9. English UI on a Windows system with German regional settings: the timesheet, the tray tooltip and the end-of-day reminder show hours with a decimal point.
