Annalo 1.5.0

## New

- **Woche vorschlagen**: at the end of the week (or any time) Annalo puts together a complete timesheet draft from what you did: meetings from your calendar, focus sessions that were not booked yet and the time you spent editing pages. Open it with „Woche vorschlagen“ in the Zeiterfassung or in the command palette. Nothing is booked twice: time you already booked, a running timer and future meetings are left out.
- **Review by day, take over in one go**: the proposal is laid out by day with the booked hours, the proposed hours and what is still missing to your daily target („Mo 2,5 h ohne Vorschlag“). Each line shows where it comes from (Kalender, Seite, Fokus), its time and length, the text and the Netzplan and Vorgang, with a badge that says how sure Annalo is and why („wie letzte Woche: Jour fixe Kunde X“, „Seite ‚Konzept Portal‘ gehört zu NP-8801/1020“). Change the length, the text or the Vorgang, tick lines or whole days on or off (space toggles, Enter takes them over) and choose „Übernehmen“ or „Alle übernehmen“. The lines become draft entries that you release and export as usual.
- **Annalo learns your Vorgänge**: the Vorgang comes from what you chose before (the last meeting of a series, a page), from a page's `vorgang:` property or that of its parent page, from what a page mentions, from earlier bookings or from similar Vorgang names. When you pick another Vorgang, Annalo remembers it for that page or meeting series, so next week's proposal is right.
- **Friday reminder**: on the last workday of the week from 14:00, if earlier days are below your daily target, a notification points to the proposal; coming back to Annalo opens it. Switch it off under Einstellungen → Benachrichtigungen → „Woche vorschlagen“. „Termine übernehmen“ in the Zeiterfassung and the gaps row of the week overview lead to the same proposal.
- **Quick capture into any page**: `>` at the start opens a page picker (type a few letters, recently edited pages first); „Neue Seite: Titel“ or Ctrl+Enter creates a page. A chip shows where the text goes, Tab switches between today's daily note, the page chosen last and the inbox.
- **Posteingang**: a page that collects quick captures with date and time, created the first time you use it. Name and default target (daily note, inbox or last page) are set under Einstellungen → Desktop → Schnellerfassung.
- **Notes for the meeting running now**: when a calendar appointment is running (or started less than 15 minutes ago), quick capture offers „Jetzt: Jour fixe Kunde X“; the text goes into the meeting note, which is created from the Kalender template if needed.
- **More than one line**: Shift+Enter starts a new line, lists and tasks keep their indentation, `[[` completes page links and `#` tags. „todo Angebot senden bis Fr“ or `due:morgen` becomes a task with its due date, shown below the field before you save.
- **Paste and drop into quick capture**: a pasted web address becomes a link with the page's title, pasted images and dropped files are stored as attachments and embedded. A new second shortcut „Auswahl übernehmen“ (off by default) opens quick capture with the marked text on Linux, or with the clipboard on Windows and macOS; otherwise quick capture offers „Zwischenablage einfügen“ (Ctrl+Shift+V) when the clipboard holds text.
- **Quick capture is instant and forgiving**: the window is prepared at start and appears in a few milliseconds with the cursor in the field. „Gespeichert in …“ links to the page and the window hides by itself. Esc keeps what you typed for next time, Ctrl+Z takes the last capture back for 30 seconds, and the last five captures are listed. When the database is busy, the text waits and is saved automatically; the main window tells you.
