Annalo 1.5.0

## New

- **Woche vorschlagen**: at the end of the week (or any time) Annalo puts together a complete timesheet draft from what you did: meetings from your calendar, focus sessions that were not booked yet and the time you spent editing pages. Open it with „Woche vorschlagen“ in the Zeiterfassung or in the command palette. Nothing is booked twice: time you already booked, a running timer and future meetings are left out.
- **Review by day, take over in one go**: the proposal is laid out by day with the booked hours, the proposed hours and what is still missing to your daily target („Mo 2,5 h ohne Vorschlag“). Each line shows where it comes from (Kalender, Seite, Fokus), its time and length, the text and the Netzplan and Vorgang, with a badge that says how sure Annalo is and why („wie letzte Woche: Jour fixe Kunde X“, „Seite ‚Konzept Portal‘ gehört zu NP-8801/1020“). Change the length, the text or the Vorgang, tick lines or whole days on or off (space toggles, Enter takes them over) and choose „Übernehmen“ or „Alle übernehmen“. The lines become draft entries that you release and export as usual.
- **Annalo learns your Vorgänge**: the Vorgang comes from what you chose before (the last meeting of a series, a page), from a page's `vorgang:` property or that of its parent page, from what a page mentions, from earlier bookings or from similar Vorgang names. When you pick another Vorgang, Annalo remembers it for that page or meeting series, so next week's proposal is right.
- **Friday reminder**: on the last workday of the week from 14:00, if earlier days are below your daily target, a notification points to the proposal; coming back to Annalo opens it. Switch it off under Einstellungen → Benachrichtigungen → „Woche vorschlagen“. „Termine übernehmen“ in the Zeiterfassung and the gaps row of the week overview lead to the same proposal.
- **E-Mail als Aufgabe oder Notiz**: turn an e-mail into a task, a note or both. Select one or more mails in Outlook (klassisch) and choose „Aktuelle E-Mail übernehmen“ in the command palette (or set a global shortcut under Einstellungen → Kalender → E-Mail), drop an `.eml` or `.msg` file anywhere onto the window, or paste the header lines of a forwarded mail („Von:“, „Gesendet:“, „An:“, „Betreff:“) into the dialog „E-Mail als Aufgabe oder Notiz…“.
- **Tasks with a due date, notes with attachments**: the task text starts as the subject without „AW:“/„WG:“; put it on the open page, today's daily note, another page or into the new note, with Heute, Morgen, Fr or Nächste Woche as due date and the priority from the mail's importance. The note lands below „E-Mails“ with sender, recipients, date and subject as properties, the text as a quote and the attachments you tick. A Vorgang for booking later and the Outlook categories as tags are optional.
- **Back to the mail in one click**: tasks and notes carry an „E-Mail“ chip that opens the mail again in Outlook (or the stored `.eml`/`.msg`). The Markdown stays clean: the chip is a normal link with the text „E-Mail: Betreff (Absender, Datum)“, which is also what the Markdown export writes.
- **Mails stay private**: mail texts never go to an AI on their own; notes from mails are marked with your privacy tag, so the assistant keeps them on the local model. „Aufgabe vorschlagen“ extracts the action and a due date only with a model of a provider marked local. Dragging a mail directly from Outlook does not work (Outlook hands over virtual files the window cannot read); use „Aktuelle E-Mail übernehmen“ or save the mail as a file.

## Fixed

- **Assistant with a LiteLLM server**: when the inline AI worked but the assistant answered with a small local model or failed, the cause was the embedding model. A chat model chosen as embedding model was asked for embeddings with every question; the server could not answer, LiteLLM paused the model for a few seconds, and the question itself was then refused („No deployments available“). The assistant now never asks a chat model for embeddings, asks a failing embedding model only once per session and searches by keywords instead, and the answer says so once.
- **Embedding model in the settings**: Einstellungen → KI offers only models that compute embeddings (LiteLLM: `mode: embedding` in the model's `model_info`, otherwise recognized by the name) and „Keine (nur Stichwortsuche)“. A chat model chosen earlier is marked, and the settings say why a model is not used.
- **Short server pauses are waited out**: when the server pauses a model for a few seconds, the assistant shows „Server kurz ausgelastet, neuer Versuch in 5 s“, asks the same model again and only then another one. „Antwort stoppen“ ends the wait. If another, smaller local model has to answer, the answer says so below the text.
- **vLLM models without tools**: a vLLM server started without `--enable-auto-tool-choice` rejects the assistant's tools. The assistant now recognizes this, answers without tools and remembers it for the model, so the next question is not rejected again.
