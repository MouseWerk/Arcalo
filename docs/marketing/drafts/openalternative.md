Arcalo is a desktop app for your workday: Markdown notes with [[links]], tasks and projects, meetings from
Outlook and ICS calendars, and time booking with /time that exports to SAP CATS, Jira worklogs, CSV or JSON.
Jira issues open and can be edited inside the app. An Obsidian vault imports as it is. AI is optional and runs
on the providers you add (Ollama, LiteLLM, OpenAI-compatible, Azure). All data stays in one local database
with no account and no telemetry; encryption, backups and Git sync are optional. Built with Rust and Tauri,
MIT licensed, for Windows, macOS and Linux.
