## Description

```
Arcalo keeps your workday in one desktop app on your own computer: Markdown notes with [[links]] and
backlinks, tasks and projects, meetings from Outlook Classic and ICS calendars, and time tracking.

Type /time in any note to book hours on an SAP PS activity or a Jira issue. The weekly timesheet exports to SAP
CATS, Jira worklogs, CSV or JSON, and "Propose the week" drafts it from your meetings and the pages you worked
on. Jira issues open inside the app, where you can read and edit them.

An Obsidian vault (with canvas files) imports as it is, and everything exports back to Markdown. An optional
assistant answers from your notes with sources, on the AI providers you add yourself (Ollama, LiteLLM,
OpenAI-compatible, Azure OpenAI), or you switch AI off completely.

No account, no telemetry. One local database with optional encryption, daily backups and optional Git sync to
your own repository. English and German. Free and open source under the MIT license.
```
