## Maker's first comment

```
Hi Product Hunt, I'm Maurice, the maker of Arcalo.

I built it for days that are meetings, notes, follow-ups and a timesheet at the end of the week, and for people
who'd rather keep all of that on their own computer than in another cloud workspace.

What you can do with it:
- Write Markdown notes with [[links]] and backlinks. An Obsidian vault imports as it is, and everything exports
  back to Markdown.
- Keep tasks in your notes, with due dates and repeat rules, and see them in one list.
- See your meetings from Outlook Classic or any ICS calendar next to the time you booked. On Windows with
  Outlook Classic you can also create and move your own appointments in Arcalo (off until you switch it on).
- Book time while you write: /time 2.5h in a note. The week exports to SAP CATS, Jira worklogs, CSV or JSON.
- New in 1.22: open a Jira issue inside the app, read it like on Jira's page and edit fields, status,
  comments and work log, with undo.
- Use an assistant on the AI providers you add (Ollama, LiteLLM, OpenAI-compatible, Azure), or switch AI off
  completely.

There is no account and no telemetry. Your data is one local database with optional encryption, backups and
Git sync to your own repository. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, in English and German, and it's free
and MIT licensed.

Two honest notes: the macOS build isn't notarized yet (the README has the one-time step), and the Windows
installer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask once.

I'd love feedback, especially from consultants who book time into SAP or Jira. What would make your Friday
timesheet easier?
```
